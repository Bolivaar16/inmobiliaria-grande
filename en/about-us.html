History

A family firm, not a franchise

We opened on Recogidas in 1970 and we are still on the same street. We know the neighbourhood, the real price behind every listing, and the neighbours who have been here all their lives.

  • 1970

    José Antonio Grande opens Inmobiliaria Grande in Granada. We have worked in the city and its province ever since.

  • Recogidas, 13

    Office on the first floor, in Granada's central shopping district. [DATO A CONFIRMAR: año del traslado a Recogidas 13]

  • 2nd and 3rd generation

    José Grande Jr. joins his father as an agent; today the family's third generation already works in the Recogidas office.

  • 2026

    We renew the brand: the Nasrid rosette, the rooster and the "Desde 1970" signature say who we are. New website, same phone numbers.

The brand

What the logo means

The rosette nods to the Nasrid tilework of the Alhambra: four diamonds around a centre, like four walls around a home. The rooster is the character, and the laurel accompanies "Desde 1970".
