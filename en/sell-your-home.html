How we work

  1. In-person valuation

    We visit the home and compare it with completed sales in your development and on your street, not with asking prices. Within 48 hours you have a figure and a report explaining why.

  2. Photo shoot and listing

    Professional photography, floor plan and our own copy. We publish it on our website and on the main property portals, and we filter the calls before they reach you.

  3. Viewings, negotiation and signing

    We only show the home to solvent buyers, negotiate with real figures and go with you to the notary. Fees agreed in writing before we start, with an invoice.
